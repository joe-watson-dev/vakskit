What's new in this direction

  • Health score first. Home opens with one number and the next step, then the details.
  • The page you're on stays pinned in the header, with a live dot and one Refresh button.
  • Underline tabs in the panel; a sidebar in the full view, with counts of things to fix.
  • SEO in five parts: Overview, Meta, Social, Structure, Export. Chips stay on screen while you scroll.
  • Plain-language checks: each one says what's wrong and how to fix it; passed checks fold away.
  • Search everything with ⌘K: every screen and action by name.
  • Settings sheet for theme and defaults, so the header stays calm.
  • History you control: remove one page or clear everything (with a confirm step), see storage used, or turn history off.
  • Full view opens in a tab and reuses it; the side panel tells you when that tab is active instead of showing an error.
  • Competitor research appears as a "coming soon" door on Home.

Try

  • Full view, then Markdown → Split.
  • SEO → Social to see the share cards.
  • First run, then Analyze this page.
  • History → remove a page, then Clear all.
